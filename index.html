Conventions that affect answers

  • Scheduling prompts state arrival, tie, quantum, and overhead assumptions when needed. A context switch is not synonymous with every kernel entry.
  • The ZyBooks ready list includes the running process. Textbook create() is distinguished from Unix fork().
  • Abstract nonnegative semaphores and signed internal counters are labeled separately. Starvation guarantees require a fair policy.
  • The primary monitor model uses Hoare-style immediate handoff and an urgent queue. Mesa/Java behavior is explicitly labeled.
  • EDF’s U≤1 test assumes independent, preemptible periodic tasks on one ideal CPU, deadlines equal periods, and no overhead or blocking. RM’s approximate 0.7 discussion is not a failure threshold.
  • Ring indices alone cannot distinguish empty from full when equal. The course elevator algorithm reverses at the last requested track.
